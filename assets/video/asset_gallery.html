The High-Stakes Astronaut Nightmare Hook
"On Earth, when a building catches fire, you run outside. Three hundred miles above Earth, you are trapped in a sealed tin can with your own fire..."
0:00 – 0:18 (18s)
WHY THIS HOOK WINS THE COMPETITION: TARGET RUNTIME: 3:38–3:40 (220s)

The Problem with other submissions: Almost every team in Flame in Freefall starts with the generic textbook opener: "Fire does not behave like Earth because of buoyancy." Judges hear this 50 times in a row and tune out.

The FLARE-X Differentiator: We open immediately on the visceral, life-or-death human reality: "On Earth, you run outside. In space, you are trapped in an airtight pressure vessel with your fire." We then introduce the hidden physical danger: flames forming silent suffocating blue domes crawling inside ventilation ducts. This hooks the judges emotionally, proves deep physical domain knowledge, and grounds the solution in 145 authentic NASA spaceflight burns.
