## 26.1 Visual Truthfulness & Epistemic Labeling

Because this submission precedes full hackathon build completion, the film adheres to strict intellectual honesty:
* Every concept screen or prototype animation displays a subtle, persistent corner watermark:
  ```text
  [ ILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT UI ]  or  [ PROPOSED INTERACTION ]
  ```
* Real NASA telemetry and archival imagery display:
  ```text
  [ NASA FLIGHT DATA / ARCHIVE ]
  ```
* Historical NASA experiments retain their official accession numbers (e.g., `BASS-II PSI-25`, `SPICE PSI-107`).
* Fabricated benchmark accuracies (e.g., "95% accuracy") and claims of live spacecraft monitoring are **strictly barred**.

## 26.4 Detailed Segment Directing & Narration Scripts

### Block 1: WHO — Hook & Identity (0:00 – 0:42 | 42 Seconds)
* **Visual Direction:** Start in deep orbital silence. The spherical flame visual provides an immediate, cinematic anchor. Transition crisply to the team identity slide. Typography must be large, high-contrast, and clean.
* **Spoken Script (84 Words):**
  > *"Fire does not behave on a spacecraft the way it does on Earth. Without gravity, hot air doesn’t rise, buoyancy vanishes, and flames form quiet, suffocating domes fed strictly by forced ventilation fans.  
  > NASA has spent over twenty years studying this phenomenon across the Space Shuttle, the ISS, and Cygnus spacecraft.  
  > We are Team FLARE-X: six researchers and engineers from Bangladesh. Our mission is to transform NASA's accumulated microgravity combustion archives into searchable, evidence-bounded fire-safety intelligence."*

---

### Block 2: WHY — The Underlying Research Bottleneck (0:42 – 1:38 | 56 Seconds)
* **Visual Direction:** Show the 5 investigation silos (BASS, FLEX, SPICE, SAFFIRE, SAME) drifting apart. Overlay the parameter tags. Briefly flash the crossed-out chatbot prompt to expose why generic LLMs fail in safety-critical physics.
* **Spoken Script (122 Words):**
  > *"As future exploration missions adopt elevated oxygen atmospheres, understanding material flammability in reduced gravity is critical. But today, NASA’s flight evidence remains fragmented.  
  > Investigations like BASS-II, FLEX, SPICE, and SAFFIRE differ in fuels, sample geometries, ventilation speeds, sensor channels, and document formats across static PDF reports and spreadsheets.  
  > When a researcher asks how a specific polymer behaves under reduced oxygen and forced airflow, finding a document is not enough. They must know: Which historical flight tests are physically comparable? What exact flame spread or extinction was observed? Does an empirical model apply? And where the evidence stops.  
  > Generic AI chatbots hallucinate numbers. Researchers need verifiable, comparable scientific evidence."*

---

### Block 3: WHAT — The FLARE-X Architecture (1:38 – 2:35 | 57 Seconds)
* **Visual Direction:** Step through the 6-stage workflow. Show natural language parsing into the structured card. Animate the adaptive family router isolating solid fuels. Display ranked BASS-II tests and the Envelope Guard gate.
* **Spoken Script (128 Words):**
  > *"FLARE-X bridges this gap through evidence-bounded scientific intelligence.  
  > A researcher enters an operational fire scenario in natural language or structured physical variables. FLARE-X parses the input, determines the combustion family, and retrieves comparable NASA flight tests.  
  > Rather than relying on simple keyword matching, our Scientific Ranker measures proximity across physical parameter space—ranking burns by material equivalence, flow velocity, and oxygen fraction.  
  > Compatible queries route to specialized quantitative models—such as our SPICE flame length regressor or FLEX droplet extinction classifier.  
  > Crucially, before any prediction is authorized, the Experimental Envelope Guard checks whether NASA has ever explored conditions similar enough to justify an inference."*

---

### Block 4: SO WHAT / NEXT — The Killer Demo & Provenance (2:35 – 3:52 | 77 Seconds)
* **Visual Direction:** The **2D Support Map** takes full screen. Animate the user star ($\star$) and the oxygen slider. Show evidence re-ranking live. Execute the **killer moment at 3:23** where the prediction surface disappears and the refusal shutter locks. Open the provenance drawer and resolve to the NASA DOI.
* **Spoken Script (168 Words):**
  > *"This is the Experimental Support Map. Your scenario appears directly alongside authentic NASA flight tests.  
  > When we adjust cabin oxygen from 21% down to 18%, FLARE-X doesn’t just recalculate a number. It dynamically re-queries the NASA flight database, re-ranks the most comparable spaceflight burns, and updates the empirical support boundary.  
  > Watch what happens as we push oxygen below 15% into untested territory:  
  > FLARE-X halts prediction. It withholds the output and marks the scenario as outside the experimental envelope. Because the absence of evidence is never evidence of safety.  
  > Clicking 'Why should I trust this?' exposes our claim-level provenance tree—separating empirical NASA observations from statistical model inferences, and tracing every finding directly to a primary NASA PSI accession and DOI.  
  > During the hackathon, we will deploy our production data pipelines and validated models.  
  > FLARE-X: Find the evidence. Know its limits."*
