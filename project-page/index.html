THE PROBLEM

NASA has the evidence. Finding the right evidence is the hard part.

Microgravity changes flame shape, spread, extinction, soot formation, radiation and material behavior. NASA has accumulated valuable evidence across many investigations, but those experiments differ in fuel or material, oxygen, pressure, flow, geometry, scale, measurements and documentation.

A researcher asking a specific fire-safety question must determine which experiments are truly comparable, what was observed, whether a quantitative model is appropriate, how far a result can be generalized, and where every scientific claim came from.

EVIDENCE-AWARE COUNTERFACTUALS

Change the conditions. Re-evaluate the evidence.

Changing oxygen, airflow, pressure or another supported parameter is designed to trigger more than a new prediction. FLARE-X reruns evidence retrieval, scientific ranking, domain validation and interpretation for the changed scenario.

EVIDENCE BEFORE ELOQUENCE

Every result has an epistemic identity.

FLARE-X is designed to separate direct NASA observations from FLARE-X model inference and AI-generated synthesis. A final Evidence Auditor checks source support, model applicability, domain status, numerical provenance and limitations before a scientific conclusion is presented.

NASA OBSERVATIONDirectly recorded experimental evidence.
MODEL INFERENCEOutput from a documented FLARE-X quantitative model.
AI SYNTHESISExplanation grounded in retrieved evidence and model outputs.
LIMITATIONConditions restricting how strongly the result should be interpreted.

SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES

Insufficient evidence is a valid result.

FLARE-X is a research and scientific decision-support concept, not certified engineering or operational safety software. NASA investigations cover specific fuels, materials, geometries and environmental conditions. Controlled experimental similarity does not guarantee equivalent spacecraft behavior.

Quantitative models are designed to remain experiment-family-specific and primarily interpolate within validated domains. Lack of matching evidence is never interpreted as evidence of safety.
